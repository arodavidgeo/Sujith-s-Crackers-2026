14 Years of Diwali Tradition

Honest Crackers Direct From Sivakasi Workshops

In 2010, Sujith witnessed families paying quadruple markups in city stalls for damp, counterfeit fireworks that sputtered out instead of sparkling. He converted his family’s Sivakasi depot into an open-counter workshop hub, guaranteeing authentic formulations, sound levels strictly adhering to national peace norms, and honest packaging.

Fourteen festive seasons later, we remain independent artisans and wholesale stockists. Every box leaving our warehouse is packed under dry Tamil Nadu sunshine, verified by handwritten parcel manifests, and cleared directly for safe family courtyards.
