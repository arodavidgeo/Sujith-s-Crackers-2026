## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authentic, trustworthy Indian festive storefront balancing traditional celebration with clear retail execution. It avoids the cacophony of typical festival posters and the sterile uniformity of generic e-commerce templates, projecting the grounded reliability of an established neighborhood cracker merchant transitioning into modern commerce.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on contemporary structured minimalism overlaid with rich festive warmth:
- Deep night-sky architectural planes ground the visual frame.
- Warm cream parchment surfaces provide an inviting, eye-comforting shopping environment that keeps product photography vibrant.
- Restrained metallic gold serves as an intentional signal of festivity, value, and action.
- Asymmetric compositions prevent formulaic catalog fatigue without sacrificing scanning speed.
- The interface emphasizes honesty: real stock statuses, transparent delivery calculations, step-by-step payment verification, and clear packaging representations.

## Colors

The color system enforces a singular, warm, disciplined palette. Pure black (`#000000`) is strictly prohibited; Charcoal Ink (`#20252B`) provides high-contrast legibility while preserving material warmth.

- **Primary (`#F4B51B` / Diwali Gold):** The single decorative and promotional accent. Used exclusively for primary buttons, active interactive indicators, promotional callouts, and key price highlights.
- **Secondary (`#10243D` / Night Navy):** Structural anchor color for high-prominence zones including the primary header, dark hero compositions, navigational drawer backdrops, and the site footer.
- **Tertiary (`#FFF9EE` / Cream Canvas):** The overall page canvas and container environment. It reduces harsh glare and grounds product cards.
- **Neutral (`#20252B` / Charcoal Ink):** Core typographical color across headings and critical content. Paired with Muted Slate (`#68717C`) for helper text, secondary meta-tags, and struck-through original pricing.
- **Pure Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Reserved for product cards, form inputs, modal dialogs, and checkout surfaces to ensure maximum image and data clarity.
- **Soft Border (`#E8E1D5`):** Structural, warm dividing lines that organize grids without visual clutter.
- **Semantic Accents:** Alert Red (`#C94232`) and Success Green (`#23834A`) are reserved strictly for system states (errors, stock levels, payment confirmation, discount pills). Never use semantic colors for decorative accents or gradients.

## Typography

The typographical strategy employs **Outfit** for all display, heading, and body surfaces, pairing it with **Geist Mono** for numeric, tabular, and transactional data.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Behavior
- **Display & Headlines:** Set in bold weights (700–800) with slight negative tracking to give headlines punch without occupying excessive vertical screen space. Never apply gradient fills, text-shadow halos, or pure black color.
- **Body:** Standard text runs comfortably at 15–16px on mobile viewports and 16px on desktop with a 1.5 line-height ratio, constrained to 60–65 characters per line to maintain effortless reading.
- **Numbers, Pricing, and IDs:** Order reference IDs, item codes, quantity tallies, discount tags, and rupee values use **Geist Mono**. This gives the storefront the practical precision of an authentic invoice or physical order pad.
- **Price Displays:** Original prices are set small, muted, and struck-through (`Muted Slate`); offer prices are enlarged, weighted at 600–700, and rendered in Charcoal Ink or Diwali Gold.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8px modular scale (`8px`, `16px`, `24px`, `32px`, `40px`, `48px`, `64px`, `80px`) within a maximum container width of 1360px.

### Grid Rhythm & Adapters
- **Desktop (1024px and up):** 12-column grid with 24px gutters and minimum 32px canvas margins. Product catalogs render in 3 or 4 columns. Promotional and hero areas use intentional asymmetric splits (e.g., 7 columns for headline/copy and 5 columns for physical product clusters).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with 20px gutters and 24px canvas margins. Product grids transition to 2 or 3 columns.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 16px horizontal margins. Product listings collapse into a steady 2-column card arrangement to optimize touch browsing. Sticky CTAs and checkout actions sit on a solid safe-area container pinned to the viewport bottom.

Layouts avoid full-viewport takeovers. Empty margins and breathing room (Density 4/10) maintain relaxed scanability during high-volume family shopping.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily through **tonal separation** and **crisp surface boundaries**, rather than dramatic drop shadows or hazy blur layers.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Cream Canvas (`#FFF9EE`), completely flat with zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) containers resting directly on Cream Canvas, delineated by a 1px border of Soft Border (`#E8E1D5`).
- **Level 2 (Hover / Elevated Surfaces):** Used on product cards during hover or active focus. Supported by a tactile, warm ambient drop: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(16, 36, 61, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(16, 36, 61, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Nav & Modals):** Top navigation bars, floating cart previews, and dialog sheets use a clean white or night-navy layer with a soft edge shadow (`box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -8px rgba(16, 36, 61, 0.12)`) and a subtle boundary line.
- **Prohibited:** No heavy blur glassmorphism, no high-intensity neon glow effects, and no skeuomorphic inner shadows.

## Shapes

The design system uses structured, moderate roundedness (Level 2).

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px – 10px):** Form inputs, quantity pickers, category filter chips, and primary buttons use consistent, accessible corner shaping that feels sturdy and modern.
- **Cards and Panels (1rem / 16px):** Product cards, checkout review cards, and dashboard modules use a unified 16px radius, softening the bounding boxes while preserving interior grid integrity.
- **Floating Promos and Hero Panels (1.5rem / 24px):** Used exclusively for large promo banners, seasonal announcements, or modal wrappers.
- **Capsule / Pill Shapes:** Reserved exclusively for small semantic tags (e.g., stock status, order stage badges, and discount indicators). Do not render main interactive buttons or content panels as full pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Solid Diwali Gold (`#F4B51B`) background with Charcoal Ink (`#20252B`) text, weighted 600. Features 10px corner radius, minimum 44px height (48px for mobile tap safety), and a subtle spring translation on press (`translateY(1px)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Pure Surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px Soft Border (`#E8E1D5`), Charcoal Ink text, and a warm cream hover fill.
- **Destructive Button:** Soft white fill with Alert Red (`#C94232`) border and text; active hover fills with a 10% tint of Alert Red.

### Cards & Catalogs
- **Product Card:** Pure White background, 16px border-radius, bordered with `#E8E1D5`. Top zone contains an un-obscured 1:1 product cutout image against a neutral surface. The lower third contains product title, Geist Mono pricing block, and a full-width compact Diwali Gold add-to-cart trigger. Badges are limited to a maximum of one promo pill and one stock pill per card.

### Chips & Filters
- **Filter Chips:** 8px radius, 36px height. Inactive state: Cream Canvas fill with a Soft Border and Charcoal text. Selected state: Night Navy (`#10243D`) background with Pure White typography.

### Input Fields & Steppers
- **Form Inputs:** Labels always remain fixed above the field in Label-MD styling (no floating labels). Input containers have a minimum height of 46px, 10px corner radius, Pure Surface background, and 1px `#E8E1D5` border. On focus, apply a crisp 1.5px ring of Diwali Gold.
- **Quantity Steppers:** Three-element segment (+, quantity, -) framed in a single border container. Numbers display in Geist Mono.

### Status Indicators & Order Milestones
- **Pills:** Compact, 24px height, uppercase Geist Mono tracking.
  - *Pending Verification:* Slate border, subtle gold dot.
  - *Payment Received / Confirmed:* Tinted green container with Success Green text.
  - *Out for Delivery / Delivered:* Solid Success Green container with white text.
- **Order Timeline:** Horizontal node chain (collapsing to a vertical spine on mobile) that accurately separates customer order submission from merchant payment validation.

### Dynamic Cart & Threshold Bar
- **Free Delivery Progress Indicator:** Clean bar component styled in Soft Border with a Diwali Gold fill gauge. Displays exact threshold copy: "Add ₹X more for FREE delivery" or "FREE home delivery unlocked" immediately upon cart modification.
