Field Safety Recommendations

Local Shop Practical Safety Guide

High-altitude sky shots create powerful visual wonder when handled responsibly. Please follow these Sivakasi safety principles rigorously.

01

Ground Placement & Bracing

Place the tube upright on hard, horizontal ground (concrete or packed earth). Never place on gravel, grass or sloped tiles. Support the tube base with two bricks on either side to prevent tipping.

02

Arm's Length Ignition

Always use a long incense stick (agarbatti) or lighting punk stick. Stand sideways, never lean any part of your body over the mouth of the tube. Light the tip of the green fuse and retreat immediately.

03

Clear Overhead Clearance

Maintain minimum 10 meters spectator perimeter. Ensure no overhead electrical wires, balcony sunshades, or dry trees within a 15-meter vertical cone above the firing point.

Keep Water Buckets at Hand In case of a non-firing shell (dud), never approach for 15 minutes. Douse thoroughly with water before disposal.
